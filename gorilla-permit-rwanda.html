How to get a gorilla permit in Rwanda

Written in Kigali by David Mazimpaka. Prices last checked: November 2026.

To trek to see mountain gorillas in Rwanda, you need a permit from the Rwanda Development Board (RDB). Without it, you cannot enter the gorilla trekking area of Volcanoes National Park. This page explains what it costs, how to book, and what to prepare.

Quick facts
  • Price for most international visitors: US$1,500 per person, per trek.
  • Time with the gorillas: one hour once you find the family.
  • Where: Volcanoes National Park, in the north near Musanze.
  • From Kigali: a drive of roughly 2 to 3 hours.
  • Who issues it: Rwanda Development Board (RDB).

How much does a gorilla permit cost?

For foreign non-residents the permit is US$1,500 per person for one trek. Rates for other groups, such as residents of East Africa, are lower. RDB has also announced special promotional rates aimed at domestic and regional visitors, so if you live in Rwanda or the region, check the current rate with RDB before you book.

The permit fee is separate from your transport, lodging, tips, and park transfers. Budget for those too.

How to book, step by step

  1. Choose your dates. The dry seasons (roughly June to September and December to February) are the most popular. Rainy months are quieter but the trails are muddier.
  2. Choose how to book. You can book directly through RDB, or through a licensed tour operator who arranges the permit with transport and lodging.
  3. Prepare your details. You will need each traveler's full name as shown on their passport, passport details, and your preferred date.
  4. Pay in full. The permit is reserved once payment is made.
  5. Keep your confirmation. You will show it at the park on the morning of the trek.

When should you book?

As early as you can, especially for June to September and holiday periods. Permits are limited each day to protect the gorillas, and popular dates sell out.

Booking directly or through a tour operator?

Directly with RDB: you control everything and deal with the official source, but you must arrange your own transport and lodging.

Through an operator: usually easier if you want one person to handle the permit, driver, and hotel. Choose a licensed company and ask exactly what is included in the price.

What to prepare before the trek

  • Sturdy waterproof hiking boots and long trousers.
  • A rain jacket, even in the dry season.
  • Gloves for gripping plants on steep, muddy ground.
  • Water, snacks, and a small daypack.
  • Travel insurance that covers cancellations.
  • Cash for tips for your guide and porters.

Anyone who is ill, especially with a cough or cold, may be refused entry, because gorillas can catch human illnesses. Ask RDB or your operator about the current health rules before you travel.

Questions

Can I buy a permit on the day?

Do not count on it. Book ahead, because daily numbers are limited.

Is the permit refundable?

Rules can change. Check the cancellation terms with RDB or your operator before paying.

Can children trek?

There is a minimum age for gorilla trekking. Confirm the current age limit with RDB before you book.
