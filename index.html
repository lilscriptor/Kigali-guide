Moto-taxi prices in Kigali

Short rides in town usually cost 500 to 1,000 RWF. Agree on the price before you get on, or use a ride app so the price is fixed.

TripTypical price (RWF)
Short trip in town (street moto)500 - 1,000
Airport taxi to city center (no luggage)4,000 - 7,000
Airport taxi to city center (with luggage)10,000 - 20,000

Prices updated November 2026.
