Kigali airport to city center: taxi, moto, bus and prices

Written in Kigali by David Mazimpaka. Prices last checked: November 2026.

Kigali International Airport (KGL) is close to the city, so you can be at your hotel within 15 to 30 minutes in normal traffic. This page compares your options so you know what is fair to pay before you land.

Quick answer
  • Easiest: an official airport taxi, a ride app, or a driver sent by your hotel.
  • Cheapest: a city bus, but it is slow and awkward with luggage.
  • Moto-taxi: cheap and quick, best only if you travel light.

Prices change with distance, time of day, and traffic. Treat these as planning ranges, and confirm the price before you start any ride.

Official airport taxis

Taxis wait at the rank outside the arrivals area, 24 hours a day. Walk to the rank rather than going with someone who approaches you inside the terminal. Tell the driver your hotel or neighborhood and agree on the price or the meter before you get in.

Ride apps

Local apps such as Yego and Move work in Kigali. There is no Uber in Rwanda. You may be able to pay with cash or mobile money, and a foreign card is not always accepted, so keep some Rwandan francs on hand. Check the pickup point inside the app, because airport pickup areas can differ from the taxi rank.

Moto-taxis

Motos are part of everyday life in Kigali and are usually the quickest way through traffic. Drivers must provide a helmet for you. They are a poor choice with a large suitcase, and you should agree on the price first or book through an app so the fare is fixed.

City bus

Buses run between the airport area and central Kigali for a very small fare. They are fine if you are traveling light and in no hurry, but routes can be confusing on your first day, and buses need a Tap&Go card, so you cannot pay cash on board.

Tips to avoid overpaying

  • Agree on the fare before you start, or use the meter or app price.
  • Exchange a small amount of cash or get a SIM card at the airport so you can pay and call a driver.
  • Send your hotel your flight number so they can arrange a pickup.
  • Keep small notes. Drivers often cannot break large bills.
  • Ask your hotel what the normal price to your area is.

Questions

How far is the airport from the city center?

It is a short drive, about 10 km to the city center, and usually 15 to 30 minutes.

Is it safe to take a taxi from the airport?

Kigali is generally considered one of the safer African capitals for arrivals. Still, use the official rank, a ride app, or a hotel driver.

Do I need cash on arrival?

Yes, bring or withdraw some Rwandan francs. Cash and mobile money are the usual payment methods for local rides.
